# Overworld tiles (placeholder art)

These are the Game Mode overworld's tiles, one image per setting family (16 px tiles, 8 to a row), with
their tile map in `packages/client/src/lib/overworld-atlases.json`. They are **placeholders**: they stand
in until Marinara Engine has its own base assets or community asset packs. A look a family's image does
not have is drawn with the overworld's plain tiles.

They are cut, combined and recoloured from these packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), all released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- Tiny Town (1.1)
- Roguelike/RPG Pack
- Roguelike Modern City
- 1-Bit Pack (settlement markers and the party, coloured per family)
- Sci-Fi RTS (rocks, scaled down)

The East Asian (wuxia), 1920s (jazz_age), science fiction and wasteland images are recolourings of the
same tiles: no CC0 top-down set for those settings was found.
